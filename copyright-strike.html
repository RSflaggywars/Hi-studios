COPYRIGHT BASICS

Copyright Strikes: What You Need to Know

A clear, practical guide to understanding copyright complaints, protecting your work, and responding carefully if you receive a strike.

What is a copyright strike?

A copyright strike is a platform action that may happen after a copyright owner submits a valid complaint alleging that content uses their protected work without permission. The exact process and consequences depend on the platform and the circumstances.

It is different from a simple automated copyright match or claim: those may affect monetization or availability without necessarily counting as a strike.

What can happen?

01

Content may be removed

A video, image, song, or other upload may be taken down or blocked.

02

Account restrictions

Some platforms limit features or apply penalties, especially for repeated violations.

03

Further legal steps

In some cases, a dispute may continue outside the platform. Rules vary by country.

If you receive a strike

  1. Read the notice carefully. Check what content is identified, who submitted the complaint, and which deadline or process applies.
  2. Keep records. Save the notice, relevant URLs, licenses, permission emails, and original project files.
  3. Review your rights. Consider whether you created the material, have permission or a license, or have a legitimate legal exception. Giving credit alone does not automatically grant permission.
  4. Use the platform's official process. A retraction request, appeal, or counter-notification may be available, but each has different requirements and possible consequences.
  5. Get qualified advice when needed. A formal counter-notification can have legal implications. Consider speaking with a lawyer if the stakes are significant.

How to reduce the risk

  • Use content you created yourself or have permission to use.
  • Check the terms of any music, image, video, or font license.
  • Keep proof of licenses and permissions.
  • Do not assume that a short clip, attribution, or a “no copyright intended” disclaimer makes use lawful.
  • When in doubt, replace the material with properly licensed or original content.

Important note

This page is general educational information, not legal advice. Copyright laws, exceptions, platform rules, and deadlines differ. Follow the instructions in your specific notice and consult a qualified professional for advice about your situation.
